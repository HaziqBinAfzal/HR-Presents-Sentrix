# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Sentrix welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, appearance, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, constructive, patient, and focused on the technical and community impact of the work. Give actionable feedback, assume good intent while addressing harmful outcomes, respect privacy, and accept responsibility for mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private information, sustained disruption, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest and protect reporter privacy.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when a person represents the project in public or private project-related communication.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, adapted for Sentrix.
